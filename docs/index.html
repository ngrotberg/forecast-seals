Forecasts of federal survey results, before they are published

Federal surveys from CDC, the Federal Reserve and the Census Bureau take a year or more to publish. These pages forecast the next published figure for every state and national group, with a likely range for each.

How it works

  • Built from public data. Every past wave of the federal survey, plus Census population data. No new survey, no personal data.
  • Every state and group, with a range. Each figure comes with the range we expect the published number to land in about four times in five.
  • Sealed, then scored. Forecasts are sealed with a dated digital fingerprint before the agency publishes, then scored against the release in public.
