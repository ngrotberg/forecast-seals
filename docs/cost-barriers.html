What this is

  • A forecast, not a survey. A statistical model reads every past wave of the public federal survey and Census population data, and forecasts the next published figure for every state and group.
  • Months ahead. You get the number before the agency publishes it.
  • On the record. Each forecast is sealed with a dated digital fingerprint before the release, then scored in public against what the agency publishes.
  • Honest about the point. For a large state the forecast usually sits close to the last measured figure; what it adds is every state and group in one place, a likely range for each, and the lead time.
